## Interpretation boundaries

- Prepared UAT, peer-review, reflection, and viva documents are not completed human assessments.
- The selected architecture is a project decision; instructor or partner approval has not been supplied.
- A local notification adapter proves local processing only, not email/SMS delivery.
- Cryptographic integrity, TLS, and backend access control have different objectives; no control establishes absolute security.
- Performance and deployment conclusions are conditional on the actual environment measured.
- The matrix was initially prepared before implementation and later updated after actual verification. These current statuses do not imply tests occurred before implementation. Git/working-tree source hashes and execution timestamps retain chronology; the final completion report records publication and remaining human activities.
